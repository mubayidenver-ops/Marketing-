Made for practical use

Use it as a guide, not a guarantee

For students, creators, professionals, small-business owners, and people learning AI tools. Adapt the examples to your task and verify important output independently.

  • AI may produce incorrect or incomplete answers.
  • Check sources, calculations, names, dates, and claims.
  • Do not enter passwords, payment details, private records, or confidential information unless your organization permits it.
