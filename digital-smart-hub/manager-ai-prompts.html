Make management work clearer, one prompt at a time.

A new, independently written prompt collection concept for preparing conversations, planning work, reviewing operations, and communicating decisions. These templates are newly drafted for this concept; they are not copied from the supplied third-party playbook.

Not affiliated with or endorsed by Claude or any AI provider.

Eight original starter prompts

Start with the situation in front of you.

Replace bracketed fields with appropriate, non-sensitive context. Check the output and apply your organization’s policies and judgment.

01 · Team communication

Prepare a clear team update

I need to update my team about [change or decision]. Use only these confirmed details: [facts]. Draft a concise message that states what is changing, why it is changing, when it takes effect, and what people should do next. Put any unanswered questions under “Needs confirmation.” Do not invent reasons, dates, commitments, or expected outcomes.

02 · Performance conversation

Plan a fair feedback discussion

Help me prepare for a feedback conversation about [work situation]. Here are specific, work-related observations and examples: [examples]. Separate observable facts from my interpretations. Suggest a respectful opening, questions that invite the other person’s perspective, and possible next steps. Flag missing evidence. Do not infer motives or make a decision about the person.

03 · Project planning

Review a project before kickoff

Review this project plan: [plan]. Identify potential risks involving scope, schedule, people or resources, dependencies, and decision-making. For each, explain the evidence, possible consequence, an early warning sign, and a practical mitigation to consider. Label assumptions clearly and ask for essential missing information. Do not present likelihood estimates as measured facts.

04 · Meetings

Improve a recurring meeting

Assess this recurring meeting using the details provided: purpose [purpose], attendees [roles], length and cadence [details], typical agenda [agenda], decisions or outputs [outputs]. Identify what seems useful, what is unclear, and two low-risk changes to test. Include a way to evaluate whether the changes help. Do not assume a meeting can be removed without checking stakeholder needs.

05 · Onboarding

Draft a role onboarding outline

Create a draft 30/60/90-day onboarding outline for [role] using these team goals and constraints: [details]. For each phase, suggest learning priorities, relationships to establish, example work, and check-in questions. Mark organization-specific facts that I must supply. Treat timelines as a starting point for manager review, not a promise of performance.

06 · Strategy

Surface assumptions in a plan

Review this proposed plan: [plan]. List its explicit and implicit assumptions. For each, state what evidence would support or challenge it, what information is missing, and what could be affected if it is wrong. Prioritize items for human investigation; do not assign numerical confidence unless I provide a basis for scoring.

07 · Reporting

Turn a report into a decision brief

Using only the data and notes below, prepare a short brief for [audience] about [topic]. State the main observed result, supporting figures with units and time period, limitations, and decisions the data does not answer. Separate observations from interpretation. Flag any calculation to verify and do not invent benchmarks or causal explanations.

08 · Change and team practices

Plan a small change experiment

We are considering this team-process change: [change]. Help design a reversible, small-scale test. Include the problem it is meant to address, who should be consulted, what to communicate, a test period to decide, possible unintended effects, and signals to review. Do not assume agreement or claim the change will improve results.

Privacy and fairness: Do not paste identifiable employee, candidate, customer, or confidential company information into an AI tool unless your organization permits it. Review outputs for bias, accuracy, policy compliance, and context before use.
